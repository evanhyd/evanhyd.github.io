I often get hungry at night when staying up late, so I asked myself, why not purchase some chocolates from the local convenience store? On a random night, I went to the school plaza and purchased 4 random chocolates.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*-MFBBExc7G5aQ_3_b5gJmA.jpeg)

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*EqJDRWdsTNRaCWHeyxxD-g.jpeg)

These are just your average (or successful) chocolate brands that you can grab from the convenience stores on the candy shelf.

## Kinder

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*OmIsP4GGNQs1W0ZQv23EFQ.jpeg)

This is one of my favourite chocolate brands along with Ferrero and Toblerone. When I was still in elementary school, my mom often purchased these three brands of chocolates whenever she returned from a business trip from Hong Kong.

The chocolate dust on the surface creates a very coarse texture. You can smell the strong milk flavour the moment you open the package. The chocolate stuff primarily contains a mixture of molten chocolate sauce and creamy milk stuffing. The chocolate itself is overall sweet and full of rich milky flavour. Love it.

## Hershey’s

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*2nco9Vl16dGgA_SUZxNPtw.jpeg)

Hershey’s chocolate texture is exactly opposite to Kinder’s. The chocolate surface is smooth and you can see the branch name embedded on it.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*ljMDG6OvNYXBq4IAG4GoFg.jpeg)

The inner stuffing, however, is just the chocolate. It has a rather uniform design compared to Kinder’s multi-layout structure. Despite the name of the chocolate being “Hershey’s Creamy Milk”, its strong milky sugary taste completely overrides the chocolate taste. The taste is similar to baby formula with lots of sugar. Overall, Hershey’s is too sweet for my taste, but I would still enjoy it if there’s any.

## Snickers

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*N0VoY6Q-WBQlJYPwyg_Jxw.jpeg)

Snickers is just Snickers and I don’t know what you would expect. I ate it in the morning before the final exam, so there aren’t any images available. The inner stuff contains caramelized brown sugar and chocolate mixed with peanuts. It has the cheapest price among all the chocolates I purchased. Overall, I wouldn’t even categorize Snickers as chocolate, but a decent snack nevertheless.

## Cadbury

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/992/1*8Es44axR9J7bs_VvqYB-nQ.jpeg)

I bought Cadury because it was on sale. Cadbury’s Milk Chocolate is nearly a one-to-one clone of Hershey’s. The chocolate has a smooth texture with its brand printed on the surface.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*SsBUHxoCTsW47hqbVpOpvg.jpeg)

But compared to Hershey’s, Cadbury has stronger milk powder tastes with less sugar. Well, what can I expect… It was labelled “MILK CHOCOLATE” on the package after all.

## Conclusion

I really like milk chocolate lol.
